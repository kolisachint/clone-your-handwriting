# Session notes — 2026-10-10

What happened, what was decided, and where to pick up.

## The goal

- A private font from the writer's own handwriting.
- Usable everywhere: web, desktop, phone, Office.
- Write on plain paper, hand over a photo. No boxes, no templates.

## Decisions

- **No templates.** An early boxed-template approach (PR #1) was
  dropped. Letters are cut from normal writing on ruled paper.
- **Keep it the writer's hand.** Fixes may finish strokes, straighten,
  resize and clean ink, but never redraw a letter.
- **Four styles** from the same strokes:
  - AnnaHand — as written, cleaned.
  - AnnaHandPaper — even pen line.
  - AnnaHandTyped — wobble smoothed, more space.
  - AnnaHandAccess — bold, wide, slashed 0.
- **Clear styles use alphabet forms** for t, g, y and I
  (sentence forms look like + or blur with l).
  Setting: `PREFER_ALPHA_CLEAR` and `PREFER_ALPHA` in `tools/make.py`.
- **A is hand-picked** (`OVERRIDE` in `tools/make.py`):
  every copy had a hurried right leg.

## Problems found and fixed

- Notebook lines stuck to letters → removed by ink tone and colour.
- Lines through o and l → removed.
- Ink blots → capped to stroke width.
- Row tilt copied into letters → each letter straightened.
- Letters sized by one fixed rule → sized to the writer's own lines.
- Pen lifted early → strokes finished to the line.
- Only alphabet lines used → sentence pages sampled too.
- `f` sat below the line, x-height metric 18% off → fixed in round 1.
